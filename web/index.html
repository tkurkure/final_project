Central Bank Stance Reader

Central banks raise interest rates to cool inflation and cut them to support the economy. This model reads central bank statements one sentence at a time and labels each one hawkish (leans toward higher rates), dovish (leans toward lower rates), neutral, or irrelevant (not about policy).

What happens when the model reads a bank it has never seen? We trained it 25 times, each time leaving one bank out, then tested it on that bank. Pick a bank to compare the model trained with it against the one that never saw it. Banks are sorted by how much the score drops.

Paste a statement or upload one as a .txt or .pdf file, then click Analyze. You get a label for every sentence and an overall score from −1 (fully dovish) to +1 (fully hawkish).

One model reads every bank the same way, so this doesn't change the reading. It only tells you how much to trust it.

The model runs entirely in your browser, so your text is never sent anywhere. The first time you click Analyze, it downloads the model (about 500 MB, once; your browser keeps it afterwards).
